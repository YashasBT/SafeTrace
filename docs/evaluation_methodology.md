# Evaluation methodology
Leak rate = complete original canary values surviving / total injected canaries × 100. Privacy score = 100 − leak rate. Partial fragments and unknown secrets are not captured by this metric.
Diagnostic score is the proportion of expected root-cause terms and keywords present in the candidate text. It is an automated heuristic, not proof of semantic correctness. All shipped cases are synthetic; real-world claims require 30–50 licensed, human-verified cases and a reviewed evaluation protocol.
